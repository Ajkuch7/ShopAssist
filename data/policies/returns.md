# Returns

Items can be returned within the category window when the order is delivered:

- Electronics: 7 days
- Headphones: 7 days
- Clothing: 30 days
- Footwear: 15 days
- Home: 10 days
- Kitchen: 10 days
- Beauty: 7 days
- Sports: 15 days

Innerwear and grocery items are non-returnable. Valid return reasons are
damaged, defective, wrong item, not as described, size or fit, and changed mind.

UPS drop-off is free. Courier pickup costs $10 unless the reason is damaged,
defective, or wrong item.
