# Exchanges

Customers can request a different size, fit, or product after the original
order passes the same return checks. The applicable return windows are 7 days
for electronics and headphones, 30 days for clothing, 15 days for footwear and
sports, 10 days for home and kitchen, and 7 days for beauty.

Innerwear and grocery items cannot be exchanged. UPS drop-off is free. Courier
pickup costs $10 unless the reason is damaged, defective, or wrong item.
